tPA / TNK Dosing

Alteplase (Recombinant Tissue Plasminogen Activator) Dosing

Total dose: 0.9 mg/kg split into a bolus dose and infusion dose over 60 minutes.

Bolus dose: 10% of the 0.9 mg/kg treatment dose

Infusion dose: 90% of the 0.9 mg/kg treatment dose delivered over 60 minutes

Tenecteplase Dosing
